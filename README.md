## 🎮 About the Game

Mall Rivals is a competitive mall simulation developed and published by Creedon Games. It entered Early Access on March 23, 2026, and received its full release on August 27, 2026. Players build shops, manage stock and employees, compete for customers, and use business tactics, voting, sabotage, and physical interactions to dominate a shared mall.

This trainer provides optional advantages for Mall Rivals, including increased cash, improved health and stamina, faster movement, easier inventory management, and enhanced store operations. ## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Cash** — Provides a large or unlimited cash balance for purchasing stores, stock, furniture, licenses, and employee services.
- **Unlimited Stamina** — Prevents stamina loss while sprinting, carrying goods, moving through the mall, or engaging in physical activities.
- **Fast Movement** — Increases walking and sprinting speed for quicker movement between stores, storage areas, vendors, and mall facilities.
- **Perk Unlocks** — Supports faster progression through the Commerce, Crime, Combat, Mobility, Social, and Labor perk branches.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Maximum Health** — Keeps the player's health at maximum during physical confrontations and dangerous encounters.

### 📌 Management

- **No Stock Cost** — Allows the player to purchase wholesale products and refill store shelves without reducing available cash.
- **Instant Store Operations** — Reduces delays when restocking shelves, handling products, serving customers, and managing shop activities.
- **Employee Efficiency** — Improves employee productivity and helps stores continue operating efficiently when the player is away.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Inventory Capacity** — Provides expanded carrying capacity for boxes, products, and other items used to operate the player's stores.

### 📌 Crime

- **Crime Protection** — Reduces the consequences of risky activities involving theft, lockpicking, stolen goods, and black-market transactions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Activate Fast Movement when you need to travel quickly between stores, storage areas, vendors, and other mall locations. Unlimited Stamina allows extended sprinting without interrupting movement. Disable movement enhancements before competitive or shared sessions.

### Tools & Inventory

Use Inventory Capacity to carry more stock and supplies while setting up or restocking a store. Unlimited Cash and No Stock Cost can be combined to test different product selections, shelf arrangements, and wholesale orders without financial limitations.

### World & Environment

Mall Rivals features procedurally generated malls, competing businesses, changing product demand, employees with individual traits, customer activity, board-room policies, informants, thieves, bodyguards, mall police, and multiple victory conditions. Use the trainer to explore these systems freely and compare store strategies without restarting for funds.

### Combat & Weapons

Health and Stamina options support encounters involving rivals, thieves, bodyguards, and police. Maximum Health reduces the risk of being knocked down, while Unlimited Stamina supports sustained movement and physical actions. Crime Protection is intended for testing risky activities and should be disabled when playing normally.
